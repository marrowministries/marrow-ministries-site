Canyon Creek Baptist Church · Temple, TX

Does Christianity Work?

II Timothy 4:16–18

Does Christianity actually work when life falls apart? In II Timothy, Dr. Marrow walks through the apostle Paul’s own story — abandoned, on trial, facing the end alone — and shows how the Lord stood with him through it all. It’s a message about the same help Paul found still being available to us today.  Watch this Sermon Now
