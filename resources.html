Before Abraham…I Am!:
The Claims of Christ from the Gospel of John

by Tim Marrow

“I AM” is Jesus’s boldest declaration about Himself. Christ Jesus says that where I am, there is God; God lives, speaks, calls, loves, chooses, forgives, suffers, and dies. In Before Abraham…I AM!, author Tim Marrow examines the seven claims of Christ as the Great I AM, demonstrating how Jesus uses simple, everyday objects to represent His claims — from the “bread of life” to the “light of the world.” Marrow helps readers understand the promises behind Jesus’s statements of “I AM,” because Christ Jesus is the eternal God, without beginning and without ending — He truly is the great I AM.
